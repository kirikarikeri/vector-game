How to play
Each turn you fire a thrust for a set duration: a fixed acceleration at a fixed angle. Example: acceleration 10, angle 0°, duration 2s pushes the turtle rightward, speeding it up for 2 seconds — it keeps that new velocity until your next move changes it. Angles go counter-clockwise: 0°=right, 90°=up, 180°=left, 270°=down. Stay inside the white track (avoid the black void and the grey infield). Pass the three amber gates in order, then cross the green finish line to complete a lap. Running off the track costs you the full move's time as a penalty and resets your velocity to zero.

About the leaderboard: this demo stores scores in this artifact's built-in persistent key-value storage rather than an external Firebase/Google Sheets project — that lets the leaderboard genuinely persist and be shared across everyone who opens this artifact, without needing you to create and paste in your own cloud credentials. If you'd like, I can swap this for a real Firebase or Google Sheets backend using your own project keys.
