How to play
Each turn, type a duration: a fixed acceleration (up to 200 m/s²) at a fixed angle (from -180° to 180°). Example: acceleration 10, angle 0°, duration 2s pushes the cat rightward, speeding it up for 2 seconds — it keeps that new velocity until your next move changes it. Angles: 0°=right, 90°=up, 180°/-180°=left, -90°=down; negative angles turn clockwise instead of counter-clockwise. Stay on the winding blue road — running off it into the white costs you the full move's time as a penalty and resets your velocity to zero. Pass each blue checkpoint circle in order, then reach the checkered flag at the end to finish. If you picked more than one run, finishing teleports you back to the start for another timed attempt, and your total time keeps adding up.

About the leaderboard: this demo stores scores in this artifact's built-in persistent key-value storage rather than an external Firebase/Google Sheets project — that lets the leaderboard genuinely persist and be shared across everyone who opens this artifact, without needing you to create and paste in your own cloud credentials. If you'd like, I can swap this for a real Firebase or Google Sheets backend using your own project keys.
